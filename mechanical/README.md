# Mechanical design files / Archivos de diseño mecánico

Suggested organization:
- `cad/`: native CAD parts and assemblies.
- `drawings/`: manufacturing drawings (PDF/DXF).
- `assembly/`: assembly references, exploded views and procedures.
- `bom/`: bill of materials and purchasing references.
- `exports/`: neutral and visualization exports (STEP/STL), when appropriate.

Do not mix released files with temporary or experimental revisions. Record the CAD software version and assembly revision.
